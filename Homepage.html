Cedarcrest Chess Team

Who are we:

We are a high school chess team based out of Cedarcrest High School in Duvall, Washington. Founded in 2025, we are a team of 16 registered members of varying skill levels dedicated to serving Cedarcrest High School and the community as well as promoting the learning of and broader participation in the game of chess.
